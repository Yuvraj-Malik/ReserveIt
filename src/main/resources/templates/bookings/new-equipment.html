How it works

  1. Requests for the same item and day queue first come, first served.
  2. The lab's faculty in-charge approves in queue order.
  3. If the day fills up, you're offered the nearest free days and can choose within 24 hours.
  4. On the day, show your QR code at the lab to collect.
  5. Late returns are strikes. Three strikes block booking for 14 days.
